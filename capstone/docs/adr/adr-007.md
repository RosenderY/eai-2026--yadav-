# ADR-007 — Confirm outcomes before acknowledging work

## Context

Once intake accepts a command, checkout must be able to wait for capacity and survive a consumer restart.

## Decision

Use saga.queue with prefetch equal to SAGA_CONCURRENCY. Persist the terminal envelope, confirm its publication, then ack. Intake subscribes to lifecycle events and checks legal transitions before saving its projection.

## Alternatives considered

A synchronous POST would couple intake availability to four providers. Acking early would need an outbox or the outcome could disappear.

## Consequences

Redelivery after a terminal write republishes the envelope. Clients may briefly read an earlier state while the lifecycle subscriber catches up.
