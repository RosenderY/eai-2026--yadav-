# ADR-008 — Deduplicate three independent identities

## Context

An HTTP retry, a second client submission and an ambiguous payment timeout can all refer to one purchase.

## Decision

Hash raw bytes under the client key. Reserve source plus order ID before the first saga step. Reuse authorize, reserve, refund and release keys at their respective providers.

## Alternatives considered

Deduplicating on correlation ID alone misses a new submission. Deduplicating only at intake cannot resolve a provider that charged before its connection failed.

## Consequences

A genuinely new attempt after a decline needs a new business order ID. Notices remain at least once because their provider accepts no idempotency key.
