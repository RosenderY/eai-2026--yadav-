# ADR-010 — Stop and inspect interrupted sagas

## Context

A process can stop after the provider has acted and before the local step record was saved. Shipping cannot be undone.

## Decision

Compare the persisted owner with a new startup UUID. Mark unfinished runs for inspection, except known pending first-step retries. Release recorded inventory before refunding a recorded payment. B2B orders never have a payment to refund.

## Alternatives considered

Automatically resuming the next call assumes the local record is complete. A wall-clock threshold alone would also flag a healthy slow provider.

## Consequences

A crash just before a call can cause unnecessary review. Check provider effects before operator compensation; missing evidence needs reconciliation. Temporal can replay completed activity history but still cannot make an external effect atomic with its completion.
